# Initiative: Partner Self-Serve Growth

## Objective
Make partner integrations easier to launch and maintain without support bottlenecks.

## Problem Statement
Partners face avoidable setup friction and low visibility into API delivery quality.

## Success Metrics
- Increase monthly active API partners by 30%
- Reduce partner onboarding support requests by 25%
