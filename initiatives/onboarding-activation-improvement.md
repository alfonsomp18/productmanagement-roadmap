# Initiative: Improve onboarding activation

## Objective
Increase the percentage of new users who complete key setup actions within their first 7 days.

## Problem Statement
Many users sign up but fail to reach first value quickly, leading to lower activation and retention.

## Success Metrics
- Increase Day-7 activation rate from 42% to 55%
- Reduce median time-to-first-value from 2.8 days to 1.5 days

## Related Features (GitHub Issues)
- #101 Onboarding checklist with progress tracker
- #102 Welcome email journey with contextual nudges
